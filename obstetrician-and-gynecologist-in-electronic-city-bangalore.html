​How Gynecology Care at Aarogya Hastha Works

​We maintain a transparent, structured process to ensure you receive precise treatment during every step of your medical journey.

Detailed Consultation & Assessment

​Your initial visit involves a thorough review of your current symptoms, menstrual history, past medical conditions, and lifestyle factors. The specialist takes time to understand your concerns and establish a medical baseline.

Diagnosis & Evaluation

​If symptoms indicate an underlying condition, the doctor organizes targeted medical tests. This may include on-site transvaginal or abdominal ultrasound imaging, hormone blood assays, glucose screenings, or a preventive Pap smear.

Personalized Treatment Plan

​Once test and scan results are available, our consultants outline a custom treatment path. This may involve medical therapy, targeted lifestyle adjustments, structured prenatal monitoring, or scheduling a minimally invasive procedure if required.

Follow-up & Long-Term Support

​Chronic conditions and ongoing pregnancies require regular evaluation. We schedule timely follow-up appointments to check your progress, adjust prescriptions, answer your questions, and provide ongoing health guidance.
