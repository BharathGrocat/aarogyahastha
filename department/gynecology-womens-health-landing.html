Yes. We offer epidural anaesthesia for painless labour. Our experienced anaesthesia team and specially trained obstetricians ensure a comfortable birthing experience while promoting natural vaginal delivery wherever possible.

Dr. Geetha R, our fertility specialist, offers comprehensive infertility evaluation, follicular monitoring, ovulation induction, hormonal treatments, and fertility-enhancing laparoscopic surgery. For complex cases, we coordinate referrals to specialized IVF centres.
