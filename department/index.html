Orthopedic Excellence at Aarogya Hastha – Your Trusted Care in Bangalore

In recent years, Bangalore has seen a rising demand for qualified orthopedic surgeons, making it one of the prominent hubs for orthopedic excellence. Fortunately, the city boasts highly experienced orthopedic surgeons recognized internationally for advanced procedures.

At Aarogya Hastha, we have a dedicated team of surgeons ensuring patient health and safety. We specialise in musculoskeletal disorders, injuries, and diseases. From open surgeries for joint replacement to sports and spinal cord injuries, our specialists are trusted by families for advanced orthopedic and trauma care. At Aarogya Hastha, our goal is to help you move without pain and return to daily life safely. As one of the best hospitals in Bangalore, we combine expert doctors, clear guidance, and modern facilities for dependable results.

We are committed to delivering personalized treatment plans tailored to each patient’s specific condition, lifestyle, and recovery goals. With advanced diagnostic technology, minimally invasive surgical techniques, and comprehensive rehabilitation support, we ensure faster recovery and long-term mobility. Our compassionate, patient-first approach means you receive continuous guidance and care at every step of your healing journey.

Conditions Treated by Our Orthopedic Specialists

Bone & Joint Care

Fractures, dislocations, arthritis (knee/hip/shoulder), bursitis, tendonitis, osteoporosis, shoulder instability, and frozen shoulder.

Sports Injuries

ACL/PCL and meniscus tears, rotator cuff injuries, shoulder impingement, ankle sprains/instability, tennis elbow, runner’s knee; arthroscopy where appropriate.

Spine & Back Problems

Cervical and lumbar spondylosis, sciatica, disc prolapse, posture-related pain, vertebral fractures.

Pediatric Orthopedics

Growth-plate injuries, developmental hip issues, limb alignment (knock-knees/bow-legs), clubfoot management, and flat feet assessment.

Hand Surgery & Microsurgery

Hand and wrist fractures, sports injuries, nerve and tendon repair, joint replacement, and complex reconstruction.

Trauma & Emergency Care

Road-traffic Accidents, multiple fractures, crush injuries, wound care, and stabilization with orthopedic triage.
